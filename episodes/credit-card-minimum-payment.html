Paying only the minimum on $8,000? The 23-year credit card math

Paying only the minimum on an $8,000 card at 22.36% APR can cost about $21,831 and take 278 months (about 23 years) in our simulation. Keeping the first minimum payment fixed at $229 instead cuts it to 57 months.

Assumptions

$8,000 balance, 22.36% APR, monthly interest = APR ÷ 12 (simplified; many issuers compound daily), example minimum = 1% of balance + interest with a $25 floor, no new purchases, no fees.
