How do tax brackets work? A $1,000 raise, $170 more income tax

Only the dollars above each bracket line pay the higher rate. In our example (2026, single filer, wages only), a $1,000 raise that crosses into the 22% bracket adds $170 of federal income tax: $60 on the half that still fits in the 12% bracket and $110 on the half above the line.
