How to read this

Each tank holds one bracket. Your taxable income (salary minus the deduction) fills the 10% tank first, then overflows into 12%, then 22%, and so on. Each tank only taxes the dollars inside it, which is why a raise can never make your federal income tax bill higher than the raise itself.

2026 single brackets and $16,100 standard deduction from IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32. Federal income tax only: no credits, no other income, no state taxes, no Social Security or Medicare. Paycheck withholding is a separate estimate. Education, not financial advice.
